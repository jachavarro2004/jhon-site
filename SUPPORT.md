# Getting help with Jhon

Jhon is maintained by one person. Pick the place that fits what you have:

- **Something wrong on this page** (a broken link, a film that does not play,
  a layout problem): open an
  [issue](https://github.com/jachavarro2004/jhon-site/issues) here. Say which
  page or section, your browser, what you expected and what happened.
- **A question or an idea about Jhon itself:** open a
  [Discussion](https://github.com/jachavarro2004/jhon-agent/discussions) in
  jhon-agent.
- **A bug in Jhon:** open an
  [issue](https://github.com/jachavarro2004/jhon-agent/issues/new/choose) in
  jhon-agent with the bug form, with your `jhon version` and runtime (Claude
  Code or pi).
- **A security problem:** never in public; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
